# Ordinary swans, interchangeable wings and herd escape

All swans now use the ordinary colour/model, including saved or received colour 2. They drop ordinary swan wings and no longer give magical-creature mana. Historical wing item IDs remain readable as ordinary wings so saved inventories and cauldrons still work.

The wings potion uses one pair of swan wings or flying-pig wings plus one golden apple, producing the existing single flask. Pigs drop one pair on death on the authoritative host; repeated death calls cannot duplicate loot. Recipe normalization accepts either source and historical saved cauldron contents. Item descriptions, Codex, ingredient shelves/shops, loading hints and prewarm match the new recipe. Both wing items reuse the existing shared feather-pair geometry/material; no new renderer/shader is introduced.

The spawn audit confirmed natural swan sites on actual generated freshwater, using the ordinary automatic Animals site scan without manually spawning the flock. Suitable water needs at least two layers and four metres of open water around the centre, outside the sea and settlement footprints. The existing deterministic site chance is unchanged.

Found and fixed two loading cases: an unloaded first sample used to stop the entire fourteen-candidate search, and incomplete shore data could reject the lake until the site was discarded. Loaded samples are now tried, incomplete data is retried, and the visibility check uses the actual lake point rather than its original grid-cell centre. Queries do not force unloaded terrain. Earth flocks also wake around guests while their host is off Earth; the former host-only Earth gate prevented this.

Nearby autonomous herd witnesses now receive a death fright even when the victim dies from the first hit. Existing defenders continue fighting; the others flee through their species' normal movement. Horses, cows and sheep run; pigs retain flight; swans can swim away if takeoff is blocked; sea animals flee underwater. An unknown killer uses the death location as the fright source instead of blaming a player. Ridden, harnessed and controlled animals keep their external controller.

Validation includes automatic flocks on real generation-31 terrain for a local player and an Earth guest with the host in space, partial-load retry cases, actual land movement after a herd death, species-specific flee states, pig wing drops/idempotence, completed cauldron brews for both wings and legacy saves, and existing herd/flight/ragdoll/horse capsule regressions. This is not an exhaustive multiplayer soak or a change in lake density.

Real browser confirmation: the scene found a deterministic natural freshwater site in the actual world recipe, streamed it, and activated two ordinary swans through Animals._scanSites without a manual flock spawn. Killing one left its mate in flee; killing a pig exercised the normal drop path. Two renderer screenshots (natural flock and interchangeable wing pairs) were inspected; no page errors. The initial software-GPU journey stalled and was stopped; the completed replay simulated/streamed normally with scene/camera matrix updates and used the actual renderer for the final images. No GPU timing claim is made. Both wing item kinds measure identically: 1 mesh, 1 shared material, 27,104 triangles, about 1.19 × 0.08 × 0.51 m at their existing item scale, exactly matching the existing plain swan-wing item.
