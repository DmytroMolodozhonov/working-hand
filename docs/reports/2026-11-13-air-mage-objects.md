# Air mage: real levitated objects

Air mages can lift up to six actual nearby loose items and physical swords/axes with Leviosa Maxima, orbit them, turn them towards an incoming threat, then launch a volley. Nothing is conjured; held items, unpaid goods, other levitation, rituals and divine gifts are excluded. The normal 50 mana plus 2/s and per-item swarm drain applies. Stun, carrying, death, disappearing caster, lost foe, recovery and empty mana release everything back to normal physics. Shared SwarmSim handles terrain contact, flight, mass/speed damage and single impact.

Host simulates movement and collisions. Existing item and weapon pose packets carry movement; bounded reliable metadata and initial snapshots identify held objects on guests. Weapon snapshots distinguish NPC levitation from player ownership. Player victims check their own Protection/gear shield before the normal armour damage path; reliable hit IDs deduplicate delivery. NPC hits use ordinary levitation damage and ward checks. Actual object bounds intercept duel charges and explosive orbs; explosions still detonate and drop nearby objects. Divine spells sweep the object aside. No new invisible actor capsules.

Validation: five direct unit cases cover real Weapon ownership, finite mana, mass damage, one hit per object, interruptions, exclusions, precise object interception, blasts, recipient Protection and duplicate delivery. Existing elemental combat (13) and stale horse capsule regression (2) pass. Actual Creative Game.frame replay lifted the dropped sword and axe plus one pre-existing item, mana fell 250→175.67 by frame 100 while other attacks also paid. After the real volley both weapon holders were null and the swarm was empty. Screenshot /tmp/air-mage-objects/air-weapons-orbit.png inspected; no browser page errors.
