# Physical air wings and elemental travellers

Some air residents and travellers have permanent physical wings. They use the existing HeroWings geometry/morphs and the player's wingStep physics, not Flight: a real run of at least 6 m/s for 1.2 seconds and multiple strokes is required. Blocked movement cannot pretend to be a run. In the air, repeated strokes, forward speed and gravity determine the flight; terrain/roof boundaries are checked in small horizontal substeps. Landing and interruption return to the ordinary NPC body physics. Wings themselves consume no mana, matching the player's wings; offensive and levitation spells retain their normal costs. The ordinary Creative air fixture still uses magical Flight so both kinds remain testable.

Wings are part of the villager look, attached to its existing torso, folded during sleep/death and disposed with the model. They add two meshes using the existing shared morph geometry/material. The shared airRun network mode and prewarm model gallery cover the new variant. One third is the deterministic variation chosen for the user's unspecified "some"; it does not affect the water mage's independently specified 25% humidity and 10% fire-immunity gifts.

Road-wizard rosters now sometimes include each elemental discipline while keeping their ordinary traveller role, name, rank and mana seed. Only travelling water mages carry a water tank; residents use actual canals. Peaceful elemental controllers yield back to the ordinary road/cart/day-night controller, so they keep walking instead of becoming stationary test NPCs. The existing guaranteed traveller scroll rules continue to apply to the unchanged traveller role.

Validation: run-up/strokes, actual obstruction, bounded landing, no Flight mana, stun, wing model lifecycle, deterministic road diversity and preserved road paths, existing wizard/traveller/elemental/earth/terrain-worker suites. Actual Creative Game.frame replay: the ordinary NPC took off after a real 1.2167-second run and three strokes; after 223 frames it was 2.5 m over its floor, mana 230 from 250 after ordinary wind attacks, knowsFlight=false. Its inspected screenshot shows the physical wings. No page errors. Prewarm construction completed 504 passes with 68 existing renderer programs and no page errors; this construction check stubs GPU draws and is not a performance benchmark or a full shader timing run.
