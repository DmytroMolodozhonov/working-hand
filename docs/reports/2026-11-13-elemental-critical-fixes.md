# Elemental regression follow-up, 13 November

User reports: black flashes near the air mage, gestures without effects, earth mage freezes in a spread stance after Bombardo near a river, missing long duel beams also against ordinary NPCs/zombies, and identical hair across elemental costumes.

Air attacks: reproduced in the real Creative game. At 17 m, five ordinary Wind casts spent mana and caused **zero** pushes because that spell only reaches 14 m. The controller now selects Maxima outside the ordinary gust's useful 12 m radius, including vertical separation. Same browser scenario after the change: five WindMaxima casts, **five** real player push hits, mana 220 → 120. Range and mana costs remain those of the existing spells.

Air picture: removed GLSL pow with a negative base (undefined inside the gust's ring). Replaced copying the default multisampled framebuffer with a direct render target and a single composite. Game draws the scene once; Ultra's own composite respects the current target. Finite projection checks, bounded projected radius, resize/dispose, and updated camera matrices. Real browser GPU check covers 24 frames, ordinary/Ultra, near-plane crossings and resize; 18 frames actually refracted. Read back every pixel: no black pixels (minimum RGB sums 371 / 312), no WebGL errors or page errors. The user's exact GPU failure is not reproduced on this software renderer; these changes remove two unsafe paths rather than asserting a hardware-specific diagnosis.

Earth interruption: stunned/thrown earth mages now cancel their old swarm, wall plan, overhead slab and pose clock once. Recovery starts a fresh lift. Actual general physics scheduling previously always served the first rag dolls before a 2.5 ms budget expired; later dolls could receive no simulation until the stun ended. Added rotating priority shared between living/dead dolls and bounded pending dt, retaining the budget and allowing sleeping dolls to check lost support. Regression simulates exhausted budget with eight dolls and proves all receive steps. The exact river scene has not yet been reproduced.

Duel picture: a charge ending during `_fly` returned before `_drawBolt`, losing its entire terminal frame. Now draws its real path up to the collision before returning, including a first-frame impact. Regression covers Jolt, Avada and Stupefy against a wall on a long frame, without drawing beyond it. This is a confirmed defect, **not proof that every instance of the user's broader missing-beam complaint has the same cause**. Real Creative casts create targeted Jolt/Avada/Stupefy charges with 200 m reach and visible segment instances over 12 m; no page errors. Added start/end journal entries for Jolt/Maxima/Avada, including direction, origin, reach and actual earth-stone blocking reason, to distinguish creation, early collision and normal immunity. Diagnostics are skipped when the journal is absent and do not change spell outcomes.

Costumes: removed fixed hair, hair colour and beard overrides from earth/water/air/fire clothing patches. Ordinary `villagerLook(seed, role, female)` now supplies the existing varied hair/skin/beards; discipline garments remain. Test verifies >10 different combinations per discipline across 60 seeds, preserving sex-appropriate ordinary variants.

Validation: 90 focused physics/earth/elemental/duel tests passed; expanded duel/ragdoll suite found a mock without targets exposed by the new diagnostics. Diagnostics now skip absent journal; affected 46 duel/Jolt tests passed. Body collision benchmark passed all floor/bed/cupboard/door cases with zero penetrations. Full start/performance soak and the exact user's GPU/river case are not claimed tested.
